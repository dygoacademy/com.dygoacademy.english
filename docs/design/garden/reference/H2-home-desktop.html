How your words grow

Dygo shows each word again just before you’d forget it, so it sticks.

1 Seed A new word. Plant it from a topic or add your own.
2 Sprout Learning. Short, frequent waterings.
3 Flower Remembered. Waterings spread out to days.
4 Fruiting Mastered. It only needs the odd visit.

Everything a growing vocabulary needs

Daily wateringReview the words that are due today. Say how well you remembered, and Dygo plans the next watering.
QuizzesChoose the word, synonyms, fill in the blank, listen and spell. Missed words come back, never a red cross.
Topic bedsIELTS Academic collections and your own beds, with example sentences, pronunciation and synonyms.
Missions and Golden BloomThree small missions a day. Finish all three for a Golden Bloom; the rain barrel keeps your streak on busy days.
Shared gardensLearn with a class or friends. See how the group is doing, never anyone’s private progress.
Your languageMeanings in Vietnamese or Chinese when you want them, English only when you don’t.
