How your words grow

Think of every word as a plant. Each time you review it and get it right, it grows, and Dygo waits a little longer before the next watering.

Stage 1SeedA new word you’ve just added.
Reviews: none yet. It’s waiting for you.Grows when: you study it for the first time.
Stage 2SproutYou’re still learning it.
Reviews: often, later that day or the next day.Grows when: you get it right a couple of times.
Stage 3FlowerYou remember it.
Reviews: every few days, then every week or two.Grows when: you keep getting it right as the gaps get longer.
Stage 4FruitingYou know it for good.
Reviews: just the odd one, weeks apart.Stays that way if: you keep getting it right. Most words get here after about four good reviews.

Common questions

Is Dygo free?

Yes. It’s free to start, and you don’t need a credit card.

Who is Dygo for?

Anyone who wants to learn English words and use them with confidence, whether for work, study, travel or everyday life.

Do I need to be good at English to start?

No. Dygo can explain words in Vietnamese or Chinese whenever you need it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in your browser on iPhone, Android, iPad and computers, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

How much time do I need?

About 5 minutes a day. Dygo shows you which words need watering today.

Can I add my own words?

Yes. Add any word to “My own words” and review it just like the others.

Is there a version for children?

A garden for kids is coming soon. For now, Dygo is designed for teens and adults.
