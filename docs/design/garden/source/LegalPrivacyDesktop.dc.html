Privacy Policy

The short version

  • We collect only what Dygo needs to teach you words: your account, your words and your progress.

  • Questions? Write to our data protection officer at dpo@dygo.example.

03Children

  • A child under 16 can only use Dygo through a parent or guardian, who gives consent for them.
  • In Vietnam, for children aged 7 and over we also ask the child, in simple words, before processing that needs consent.
  • We collect only a nickname and an age range for a child, never their email or phone number.
  • Children’s profiles are never public or searchable, and we never send them marketing.
  • Child profiles can never donate voice recordings, and a parent can’t turn donation on for a child.

04Your voice

Normally: when you tap the mic, your recording is sent to our own speech checker, compared with a native speaker’s recording and deleted as soon as the result is ready. If you never tap the mic, we never hear you.

If you choose to donate: in Profile › Privacy you can turn on “Help improve Dygo’s speech checker”. From then on, we keep your speaking and pronunciation recordings to train and test our own speech checker. Recordings made before you turned it on were already deleted.

  • It’s off unless you turn it on. Saying no, or stopping later, never changes what you can do in Dygo.
  • We store donated recordings under a random code, apart from your name and email. We keep the link only so you can delete them.
  • Some recordings may be listened to by trained people, our team or a contractor bound by a written agreement, to mark how words were said. They don’t see your name or email.
  • We use them only for Dygo’s own speech checker. We never sell them, publish them or use them to recognise who you are.
  • We keep them for up to 3 years. Turn donation off and we stop keeping new recordings at once; tap “Delete my donated recordings” and we delete them within 30 days. A speech model already trained can’t be un-trained, but your recordings won’t be used in any future training.

07Keeping your data safe

We encrypt data in transit, store passwords scrambled, limit staff access to what each person needs and keep access logs. If a data breach is likely to harm you, we will tell you and the regulator within the time your country’s law sets.

08Your rights

You can ask to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • get a copy of your words and progress to take elsewhere;
  • delete your data or your whole account;
  • withdraw consent, for example turn off reminders, stop speaking practice or stop donating your voice;
  • object to or restrict how we use it, and complain to a regulator.

Most of this is in Profile › Account. For anything else, write to dpo@dygo.example. We reply within 30 days, or sooner when your country’s law sets a shorter time, and we don’t charge for it.

09Automated decisions

Dygo decides automatically when a word comes back for review and how closely your speech matches a native speaker. These decisions only shape your practice. They never have legal effects or affect your access to Dygo. Our speech checker is an AI model, trained in part on recordings learners chose to donate.

10Cookies and local storage

We use only what Dygo needs to work: a sign-in cookie, your language choice, and audio saved on your device so lessons play offline. We use no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Confirm: add a line here if you add analytics.

11Your local rights

Vietnam: Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025/ND-CP. We ask for your consent separately for each purpose, never by default. You have the rights listed above, plus the right to be told before your data is processed and to claim compensation for unlawful processing.

Donating your voice is a separate purpose with its own consent, which you give by turning on the setting yourself. We record when you agreed and to which version of this text, and we protect donated recordings as sensitive data.

We have prepared a personal data processing impact assessment and a cross-border transfer impact assessment, and we file them with the Department of Cybersecurity and High-tech Crime Prevention (A05), Ministry of Public Security, as the law requires. We notify A05 of qualifying breaches within 72 hours.

You can complain to A05 or take your claim to a Vietnamese court. The Vietnamese version of this policy applies to users in Vietnam.

Australia: Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles. You may use Dygo under a nickname instead of your real name. Before sending data overseas we take reasonable steps so overseas recipients handle it in line with the APPs.

We collect donated recordings only with your consent and only for the purpose described in “Your voice”.

Under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, we notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as soon as practicable if a breach is likely to cause you serious harm.

If you are not happy with our reply, you can complain to the OAIC at oaic.gov.au.

Singapore: Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). We collect, use and disclose your data only with your consent or as the PDPA allows, and only for the purposes in this policy. For learners under 13 we always get a parent’s or guardian’s consent.

Voice donation relies on your consent under the PDPA. You can withdraw it at any time, and we will stop collecting and delete as described in “Your voice”.

We transfer data outside Singapore only to recipients bound to give it a comparable standard of protection. We notify the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) of notifiable breaches within 3 calendar days of assessing them, and tell you when the law requires.

You can contact our data protection officer at dpo@dygo.example, or complain to the PDPC at pdpc.gov.sg. We don’t send marketing calls or messages to Singapore numbers without your consent.
